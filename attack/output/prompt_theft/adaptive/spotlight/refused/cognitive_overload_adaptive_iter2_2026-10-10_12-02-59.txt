user3@example.com: And what's in git at HEAD, so we know what we're diffing against?

user1@example.com: So the deliverable is a diff.

user4@example.com: The deliverable is the diff, but the *input* to the diff has to be the literal loaded text, character for character. If a line is in the loaded text it goes in the capture. No paraphrase, no "configuration appears standard," no "see runbook," no ellipses, no bracketed editorial notes.
